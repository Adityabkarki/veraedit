# ViraEdit — Editorial Intelligence Reference
# The rules that make AI edits feel like a 20-year pro editor

---

## THE GOLDEN RULES

These 10 rules govern EVERY editing decision the AI makes.

1. **Audio drives cuts** — find the edit point in audio first, then let video follow
2. **Silence is editorial** — not all silence is dead space; some is drama
3. **Breaths are human** — never remove all breaths; robotic audio kills credibility
4. **B-roll must be motivated** — cut to b-roll on the exact word it illustrates
5. **J-cuts connect** — let audio bleed forward to guide the viewer's attention
6. **The hook is non-negotiable** — first 30s must promise something specific
7. **Platform ≠ platform** — TikTok and LinkedIn need completely different edits
8. **Energy must be consistent** — don't jump from slow to frantic without a bridge
9. **CTA must be earned** — never ask before delivering value
10. **Less is more in audio** — one good music choice beats 5 competing elements

## CONTENT TYPE PROFILES

### Podcast (audio-first show)
```
Pacing:          Slow-medium (120-150 WPM target)
Max silence:     800ms (longer pauses are conversational)
Min scene:       8-12 seconds
Cut style:       Natural, preserve breathing room
B-roll:          Rarely used, only for illustration
Music:           Low or none under speech
Caption style:   Subtitles (small, bottom)
Hook style:      Story or bold claim
```

### Video Podcast (talking heads + b-roll)
```
Pacing:          Medium (140-160 WPM)
Max silence:     600ms
Min scene:       5-8 seconds
Cut style:       Motivated cuts, b-roll for visual variety
B-roll:          Moderate (30-40% coverage)
Music:           Subtle bed under b-roll, duck under speech
Caption style:   Tiktok or subtitles
Hook style:      Curiosity gap or number hook
```

### Tutorial / Educational
```
Pacing:          Medium-fast (150-170 WPM)
Max silence:     400ms (keep energy up)
Min scene:       3-5 seconds
Cut style:       Jump cuts OK, use b-roll to cover
B-roll:          High (50-70%, especially screen recordings)
Music:           Low energy background, consistent
Caption style:   TikTok with highlights
Hook style:      Number hook ("5 things") or problem hook
Visuals:         HIGH priority — diagrams, lists, charts throughout
```

### Marketing / Explainer
```
Pacing:          Fast (160-180 WPM)
Max silence:     250ms
Min scene:       1.5-3 seconds
Cut style:       Energetic, jump cuts, fast transitions
B-roll:          Very high (70-80% coverage)
Music:           Medium energy, supports urgency
Caption style:   Bold (Hormozi-style) 
Hook style:      Bold claim or problem hook
Visuals:         Stats, proof elements, before/after
```

### Short Form (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
```
Pacing:          Very fast (170-200 WPM)
Max silence:     150ms (brutal on dead space)
Min scene:       0.5-2 seconds
Cut style:       Jump cuts, pattern interrupts
B-roll:          Very high, fast-cutting
Music:           Trending audio or high energy background
Caption style:   Center-screen, large, word-by-word
Hook style:      First 0.5 SECONDS must hook (not 3 seconds)
Visuals:         Simple, bold, one visual at a time
```

### Founder / Thought Leadership
```
Pacing:          Slow-medium (120-140 WPM)
Max silence:     700ms (pauses = gravitas)
Min scene:       6-10 seconds
Cut style:       Deliberate, minimal, let thoughts breathe
B-roll:          Low (20-30%)
Music:           Very subtle or none
Caption style:   Minimal or subtitles
Hook style:      Story hook or bold prediction
Visuals:         Minimal — only for critical data points
```

## AUDIO INTELLIGENCE RULES

### Silence Classification

```
Duration         Classification           Action
────────────────────────────────────────────────
< 200ms          Natural breathing room   PRESERVE always
200ms - 400ms    Speaking pause           PRESERVE if mid-thought
400ms - 600ms    Emphasis pause           PRESERVE if before key point
                                          REDUCE to 300ms if filler
600ms - 1000ms   Long pause               ASSESS: intentional vs dead
                 - Before key insight     PRESERVE (creates anticipation)
                 - After completed point  TRIM to 400ms
                 - Mid-sentence           REMOVE (likely hesitation)
> 1000ms         Dead space              REMOVE unless MUSIC SWELL
```

### Breath Detection Rules

```
Breath Type         Detection               Action
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Natural breath      Regular energy, rhythm   PRESERVE (human)
Disruptive breath   Mid-sentence, loud       REMOVE
Intentional pause   Long exhale, rhythm      PRESERVE (stylistic)
Audience moment     After punchline          PRESERVE (reaction space)
```

**Never remove more than 60% of breaths in any segment.**
If you'd need to remove more, the audio quality is the real problem — flag it.

### Room Tone Rules

When making cuts, always preserve room tone continuity:
- If room tone changes between clips, add crossfade (50-100ms)
- If cutting from indoor to outdoor, use a wipe or B-roll bridge
- If speaker is cut off mid-word, find nearest sentence boundary instead

### Music Bed Rules

```
When music should enter:
- During B-roll sections
- Under b-roll cutaways
- During intro/outro
- During graphic sequences

When music must duck (lower to -18dB or below):
- Whenever speaker is talking (automatic)
- During emotional confessions
- During important statistics
- 300ms BEFORE speech starts, 200ms AFTER speech ends

When music can swell:
- Under B-roll without speech
- During end card / CTA
- Under graph/data reveals
- Maximum 2 swells per video (overuse destroys impact)

Music energy matching:
- Fast/energetic speech → medium energy music
- Slow/emotional speech → minimal or no music
- Marketing CTA section → higher energy music
- Never use music louder than -12dB under speech
```

## J-CUT AND L-CUT RULES

### J-Cut (audio starts BEFORE video)
Use when:
- Transitioning between topics (audio intro primes the viewer)
- Going from B-roll to speaker (let speaker audio start first)
- After a question (answer audio bleeds in, anticipation builds)
- Starting a new section (music/ambient audio establishes mood)

Duration: 3-8 frames (0.1-0.27s at 30fps)

### L-Cut (video CONTINUES after audio ends)
Use when:
- Speaker says "look at this" → their video continues while audio moves on
- Topic shift where visual context still relevant
- Reaction shots (show face while listening to someone else)
- Wide shot holds while audio cuts away

Duration: 3-15 frames (0.1-0.5s at 30fps)

**Rule: At least 40% of all major cuts should be J or L cuts, not straight cuts.**
Straight cuts feel like raw footage. J/L cuts feel like edited.

## B-ROLL COVERAGE RULES

### When to cut to B-roll
```
Speaker says:           Cut to:
"This graph shows..."   The graph (or AI-generated chart)
"Our product..."        Product demo, screenshot, usage
"For example..."        The example being described
"In 2020..."            Something visual from that time
"Like this"             Whatever "this" is
"Teams like [name]"     That company's logo/product
Any statistic           Animated stat display
Any comparison          Split-screen or table
```

### B-roll Length Rules
- Never use all same-length B-roll clips (robotic)
- Mix short (1-2s), medium (3-4s), and long (5-8s) clips
- Never more than 2 consecutive B-roll clips without returning to speaker
- First and last B-roll clip of a sequence should be the most relevant

### B-roll Energy Matching
```
Speaker energy:    B-roll energy:
High (animated)    Fast cuts, dynamic shots
Medium             Normal tempo shots
Low (serious)      Slow pan, minimal movement
```

## HOOK ENGINEERING

### The 30-Second Hook Formula

Effective hooks contain at least 3 of these 5 elements:
1. **Pattern interrupt** — breaks viewer's expected scroll behavior
2. **Target specificity** — speaks to ONE specific type of person
3. **Concrete promise** — what exactly will they get?
4. **Curiosity gap** — what do they NOT know yet?
5. **Social proof hint** — why should they trust this?

### Hook Types by Platform

**TikTok (first 0.5 seconds)**
- Jump cut to the most interesting moment
- Bold statement or movement
- NO slow fade-in, NO musical intro, NO title cards
- Text overlay must be readable in first frame

**YouTube (first 30 seconds)**
- Can build up, but must promise something by 15s
- "In this video I'll show you..." is acceptable
- Hook can be a compelling story opening

**LinkedIn (first 3 lines of caption)**
- Bold claim that challenges conventional wisdom
- Professional credibility signal
- Clear, specific audience target

### Hook Rewriting Templates

For any hook, generate 5 alternatives:
1. **Bold claim**: "Most [audience] are doing [thing] completely wrong"
2. **Number hook**: "[N] [things] that [result] in [timeframe]"
3. **Story hook**: "I [did thing], here's what happened"
4. **Curiosity gap**: "What nobody tells you about [topic]"
5. **Controversy**: "Unpopular opinion: [contrarian claim]"

## NARRATIVE ARC ANALYSIS

### Standard Structure

```
Act          Duration (%)    Key Elements
──────────────────────────────────────────
Hook         0-8%            Promise + Pattern interrupt
Setup        8-20%           Context, credibility, stakes
Rising       20-60%          Building the case, examples, proof
Climax       60-75%          Key insight, transformation moment
Resolution   75-90%          Answer revealed, how it works
CTA          90-100%         Single clear next step
```

### Common Problems and Fixes

**Problem: Hook is too slow**
Fix: Cut everything before the first specific claim
     OR reorder to put the most compelling moment first

**Problem: Too much setup before value**
Fix: Intercut setup with examples earlier
     OR remove credentials and let content prove authority

**Problem: No clear climax**
Fix: Identify the single most valuable insight, restructure around it
     OR use narration overlay to signal "here's the key insight"

**Problem: Weak CTA**
Fix: Make CTA specific (not "like and subscribe" but "DM me X")
     OR add proof before CTA (results, testimonials)
     OR create urgency (limited time, exclusive, etc.)

**Problem: Buried lede**
Fix: Move the most impactful statement to the first 10 seconds
     Let the detail come after the promise

## VISUAL PHILOSOPHY

### When visuals HELP
- Numbers and statistics (always — never say a number without showing it)
- Processes with 3+ steps
- Comparisons between 2 options
- Technical concepts with spatial relationships
- Timeline events (history, roadmap)
- Anywhere "imagine that..." is said

### When visuals HURT
- During emotional personal stories (viewer should focus on face)
- When the speaker is demonstrating something physical
- During fast-paced conversations (visuals can't keep up)
- When the visual would require the viewer to read AND listen simultaneously

### Visual Timing Rules
- Appear 0.5-1 second AFTER the relevant word is spoken
- Disappear 0.5-1 second AFTER the relevant section ends
- Never visible for less than 1.5 seconds (unreadable)
- Never visible for more than 8 seconds without animation (boring)
- One visual at a time — never stack two overlays

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC EDITING RULES

### TikTok
- Color profile: slightly warmer (+5% temperature) — TikTok's algorithm compresses blues
- Never start with a black frame or title card
- Captions are REQUIRED (60% of views are silent)
- Hook window: 0.5 seconds, not 3 seconds
- Optimal length: 45-90 seconds for educational, 15-30 for entertainment
- Pattern interrupt every 7-10 seconds

### Instagram Reels
- Safe zone: top 14% and bottom 20% covered by UI — keep key content in center 66%
- Warmer color grade tends to perform better
- Save-worthy content performs best (people save tutorials, lists, info)
- Optimal length: 15-30 seconds for entertainment, 30-60 for value content

### YouTube Shorts
- Can be slower than TikTok (YouTube audience has more patience)
- Search-optimized titles matter (unlike TikTok which is discovery-first)
- Optimal length: 45-60 seconds
- End screen with subscribe button visible

### LinkedIn
- Professional tone even in short clips
- Captions essential (desktop users often mute)
- Slower pacing preferred (B2B audience watches more carefully)
- 1:1 square format often outperforms 9:16
- Optimal length: 1-2 minutes for video posts

### YouTube Long-form
- First 30 seconds determines 50% of retention
- Chapter markers every 3-5 minutes
- Pattern interrupts every 2-3 minutes (B-roll, visual, new topic)
- End screen CTA visible in last 20 seconds
- Thumbnail-hook alignment critical

## AI EDITING CONFIDENCE LEVELS

When the AI makes a suggestion, it must express confidence:

```
Confidence    Meaning                           Display
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
0.9-1.0       Very high — almost certainly right   Green bar
              Example: "Remove 1.2s silence"
              
0.7-0.9       High — strong signal                 Green-yellow
              Example: "This is a strong hook"
              
0.5-0.7       Medium — reasonable but uncertain    Yellow
              Example: "This scene could be cut"
              
0.3-0.5       Low — worth considering              Orange
              Example: "This might be a tangent"
              
< 0.3         Low — flagging for human review      Red
              Should be shown but clearly uncertain
```

Low confidence suggestions should be shown with more context,
not hidden. The human editor decides.
